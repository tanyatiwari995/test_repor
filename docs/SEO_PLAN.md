## 2. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) Framework
- **Entity Schema Injection:** Inject `Organization`, `Service`, `FAQPage`, and `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD on all pages.
- **Direct Fact & Statistic Blocks:** Provide quantifiable metric highlights (e.g. 99.5% accuracy, sub-2s load time) to encourage LLM citations.
- **Structured Q&A Sections:** H2/H3 question headers followed immediately by direct answer paragraphs for ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.
